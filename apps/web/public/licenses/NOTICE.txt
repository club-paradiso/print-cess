  https://github.com/hoppergee/heic-to
  Converts HEIC photos to JPEG in the browser. It is loaded unmodified as a
  separate, dynamically imported module (heic-to/csp), so it can be replaced

libvips, through sharp (LGPL-3.0-or-later)
  https://github.com/libvips/libvips
